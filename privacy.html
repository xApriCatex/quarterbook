Privacy policy

For the Quarterbook iPhone app. Last updated 8 November 2026.

The short version

Quarterbook collects no data. There is no account, no sign-in and no bank connection, and we never see, sell or share your records.

Who we are

Quarterbook is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: apricate00@gmail.com.

Where your records are kept

Your entries, income sources and settings are saved in Quarterbook's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. If your phone makes its own backup (for example iCloud Backup), that backup includes them, under your Apple account's privacy terms.

When anything leaves your phone

  • Only when you choose to share a file: a quarter pack, a spreadsheet of entries or a backup file. You pick where it goes in the share sheet (email, Files, your filing software), and what happens to it there is up to you and that app.
  • Quarterbook does not send anything to HMRC. You send your quarterly update with your own filing software.

Permissions

Quarterbook asks for no permissions. Restoring a backup uses the iPhone's own file picker, which shows the app only the file you choose.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Delete everything, removes your records and the app's own copies of any exports from your iPhone; deleting the app does the same. Files you have already shared or saved elsewhere (email, Files, your filing software) and your phone's own backups are not touched; delete those where they are. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete. HMRC expects business records to be kept for at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline for each tax year, so save a backup first if you may need them.

Children

Quarterbook is for people running a business or letting property, is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Your rights
