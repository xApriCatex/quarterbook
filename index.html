Quarterbook

Making Tax Digital records for UK sole traders and landlords, on your iPhone. Log money in and out in HMRC's own categories, see each quarter's figures and deadline, and export the quarter for your filing software.

Common questions

Does Quarterbook send my update to HMRC? No. It keeps your records and exports each quarter's figures as a spreadsheet (the quarter pack). You send the update with bridging software that reads the spreadsheet; GOV.UK lists the software HMRC recognises, and some of it is free.

Which categories does it use? HMRC's own categories for self-employment and UK property, in plain words. The quarter pack shows each one with its box number and the field name MTD software expects.

A trade and a let flat? Add each as an income source. Each keeps its own figures and quarter pack. HMRC treats all your UK lets as one property business, and so does Quarterbook.

What is the set-aside figure? A share of your profit you choose in Settings, to help you save for your tax bill. It is a rough guide, not a tax calculation.

Where are my records? On your iPhone, and in your phone's own backup if you use one. Quarterbook has no account and never uploads them. Use "Save a backup" in Settings now and then and keep the file somewhere safe; HMRC expects records to be kept for at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline for each tax year.

Moving to a new phone: save a backup in Settings on the old phone, then restore it in Settings on the new one.

Not tax advice: Quarterbook is a record keeper. Check anything you are unsure of with HMRC or an accountant.
